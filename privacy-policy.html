Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Tim Begander ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Embodied Cognition (the "App") on Android.

Privacy at a glance: Embodied Cognition does not require an account. Vocabulary lists and learning progress are stored locally on your device. When you request AI-powered features, selected photographs or vocabulary word pairs are transmitted to OpenRouter and may be processed by third-party AI model providers. These requests are not intentionally associated with your name, email address, or an App-specific user identifier.

Information we collect

The App does not require registration and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or account identifiers.

  • Vocabulary and learning data: Your vocabulary lists, word pairs, and learning progress are stored locally on your Android device.
  • Photographs: When you choose to import vocabulary from a photograph, the selected image is sent to OpenRouter for AI-assisted text recognition and extraction of vocabulary pairs. Images may contain personal information if it appears in the photograph.
  • Vocabulary for AI exercises: When you request a fill-in-the-blank exercise, the relevant vocabulary word pair is sent to OpenRouter to generate a suitable sentence.

We do not operate a server that stores submitted photographs or vocabulary requests. However, OpenRouter and downstream AI providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata. Requests are sent without intentionally including your name, email address, or an App-specific user identifier. This does not guarantee complete anonymity.

How we use information

Information is processed to provide vocabulary learning features, recognize vocabulary from user-selected photographs, and generate fill-in-the-blank learning exercises based on vocabulary word pairs. We do not use submitted content for advertising or sell it.

Permissions

The App uses internet access to communicate with OpenRouter for AI-powered features. It may use Android's photo selection or camera functionality when you choose to capture or import a vocabulary photograph. Any permissions requested for these features can be managed in Android settings; disabling them may prevent the corresponding feature from working.

Sharing and third-party services

When you use AI-powered features, the App sends selected photographs or vocabulary word pairs to OpenRouter, which routes requests to third-party AI model providers. OpenRouter and the selected providers may process the submitted content and associated technical metadata to fulfill the request. Their logging and retention practices can vary by provider and model.

For more information, see the OpenRouter Privacy Policy and OpenRouter documentation. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Vocabulary lists and learning progress remain on your device until you remove them or clear the App's data. Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored data, subject to Android backup and restore functionality.

We do not retain copies of AI submissions on developer-operated servers. OpenRouter and downstream model providers may retain request data or logs under their own policies. Because requests are not intentionally linked to an App-specific user account, it may not be possible for us to identify or delete an individual submission held by those providers. For questions or privacy requests, contact tim.begander@gmail.com.

Security

Communications with OpenRouter use encrypted HTTPS connections. The App is designed to transmit only the content needed for the requested AI feature. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

Embodied Cognition is an educational vocabulary learning App that may be used by children. The App does not require children to register or provide their names or email addresses. Photographs and vocabulary content are submitted to external AI services only when the corresponding features are used. Parents, guardians, and users should avoid submitting photographs containing personal or sensitive information, particularly information about children.

Your choices and rights

You can use the App's local learning features without submitting photographs or vocabulary pairs for AI processing. You can manage available permissions in Android settings and remove locally stored data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact tim.begander@gmail.com to exercise these rights. Requests involving third-party AI providers may be subject to their ability to identify the relevant information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will publish the revised version at this location and update the date above. If a change materially affects your privacy, we will provide additional notice where required.
